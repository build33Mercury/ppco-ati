# Data and atlas policy

The ATI Reference code-only package has no ABIDE/HCP/CoRR participant data, atlas NIfTI images, or derived atlas operator matrices. The six `source_data` CSVs are historical aggregate figure-level tables only; they do not permit an independent participant-level replay. Software BSD-3-Clause terms do not confer redistribution rights for third-party data and atlases. Operators must be acquired or built with independently verified permissions and preprocessing versions.
