# Data availability and rights

This release contains reference code, software tests, synthetic examples, finite-operator identity hashes, six aggregate figure tables and small aggregate numerical evidence files.

**Not included:** participant-level HCP, ABIDE or CoRR datasets; atlas NIfTI image bytes; original derived atlas operator matrices; source participant masks/time series; or a complete raw-to-figure replay of the published or submitted manuscript's historical analyses. `FROZEN_OPERATOR_IDENTITIES.csv` documents historical digests and dimensions only.

Authorized users must obtain source imaging and atlases from their original providers under the applicable terms. Reconstructing a similarly named atlas or obtaining a different processing release is not evidence that its bytes or effective observation operator are identical to the historical analysis. Operator/preprocessing identity and legal redistribution rights must be separately checked.

No atlas image, derived restricted operator, third-party participant data, or other provider-controlled material is licensed for redistribution by this repository's BSD software license. See [License scope](LICENSE_SCOPE.md) and [Third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The aggregate figure files alone do not support an independent subject-level HCP/ABIDE replay. The scientific paper must disclose this limitation and the scope of its data access and ethics basis separately.
